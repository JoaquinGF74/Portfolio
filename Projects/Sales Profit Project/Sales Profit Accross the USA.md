# **Sales Profit Accross the USA**

![Sales Profit Accross the USA](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ondrej-dusa/Portfolio/main/assets/sales_dashboard_img.png)

### **Project Overview**

This project focuses on analyzing and visualizing sales information using **Excel** and **Tableau**. The goal was organize, process, and analyze sales from different estates, display profit, and create an interactive **Power BI dashboard**  

# **Objectives**

- **Data Processing:** Establish both data sources, and interconnect both tables (Orders & Returns).
- **State-Based Sales:** Showcase Sales by State and their respective profit margin per Segment & Category.
- **Data Aggregation:** Summarize key metrics such as Total Revenue, Profit, Profit Ratio, Discount, and more.
- **Interactive Dashboard:** Develop a Tableau dashboard to visualize key sales trends. 

## **Methodology**

### 1. Data Organization

- In the first Sheet, hierchical fields are performed to facilitate data organization and usage. This also allows the connections between fields in their respective hierarchy. 

### 2. Tooltips Design

- Tooltips were made that helps the dashboard display key information more effectively such as profit by city on each State. 

### 3. Tableau Dashboard

- Visualized Executive Overview, Total Revenue, Orders, and Profitability through a Map Chart for profit analysis.
- Created Area Charts to visualize profit month by month.  

## Key Insights

- Profitability in the 4-year span is of 12.5%, indicating good revenue that allows expansion and growth. The most profitable states were Maine, Delaware, Arkansas, and Minnesota, showcasing a 30% profit on each state.
- The Technology Category displays as the most profitable with a profit of 17.4% YoY. Companies looking to invest should prioritize branching into the technology field.
- Seasonal trends can be observed. With the holidays and festivites at the end of the year, the Q4 of each years shows to be the most profitable, achieving Profit Ratios of 42.1%. 

### Technologies Used

- `Excel`: Data Organization, cleaning, and interpretation.
- `Tableau`: Data vsiualization and dashboard creation.
